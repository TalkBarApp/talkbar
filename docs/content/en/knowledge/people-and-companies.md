**People** are your contacts and the speakers TalkBar has met in your sessions. **Companies** are the organizations they belong to, with your relationship to each one. TalkBar uses both to label speakers, to answer chats and to show context during sessions.

Open **Knowledge → People** or **Knowledge → Companies**.

## People

The People tab lists everyone TalkBar knows in this workspace. Each card shows the person's name, role, company, email and when you last talked.

::screenshot{src="/screenshots/knowledge/people-tab.png" alt="Knowledge → People tab with the All/Confirmed/Unconfirmed tabs, company and relationship filters, the Cards view/Sessions view toggle, and a grid of person cards, one marked Unconfirmed" caption="Knowledge → People"}
::

### Where people come from

- **You add them.** Click **Add person**.
- **You name a speaker.** When you name a speaker in a session, that person is linked to the session and appears here. See [Speakers](/voice/speakers).
- **Knowledge scans suggest them.** A scan can suggest a new person or new details for someone you have. You approve them in [Review](/knowledge/review-and-scans).

### Confirmed and unconfirmed

**Confirmed** people are ones you know. **Unconfirmed** people were found automatically and may still need a name. Confirm a person so speaker assignment recognizes them first in future sessions.

To confirm someone, open their menu and choose **Mark confirmed**, or tick **Confirmed person** when you edit them. **Mark unconfirmed** reverses it.

### Person details

Click a person to open their details:

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Details** | Email, phone, relationship tag and notes. |
| **Company** | Their current company and role, and earlier ones. Old sessions keep the role and company from the time of the call. |
| **Projects and series** | Projects and session series the person was added to. |
| **Sessions** | Every session they took part in, and the speaker name they spoke as. |
| **Related** | Linked knowledge items, such as memory cards. |
| **Timeline** | Changes to this person over time. |

To change a person, click **Edit**:

| Field | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Photo | **Upload photo**, **Change photo** or **Remove photo**. |
| **Name** | The person's name. |
| **Role** | Their job title, for example "VP Operations". |
| **Company** | Pick a company, or **Add new company**. |
| **Email**, **Phone** | Contact details. |
| **Relationship tag** | Your own tag, for example "Economic buyer". |
| **Notes** | What you should remember about this person. |
| **Confirmed person** | Uncheck to mark the person as unconfirmed. |

### Find and organize people

- Tabs: **All**, **Confirmed**, **Unconfirmed**.
- Filter by **Company** or by **Relationship** tag. **Search people…** finds people by name.
- **Show archived** includes archived people.
- **Cards view** shows people as cards. **Sessions view** groups them by the sessions they were in.
- **Pin** keeps a person at the top. **Pinned on top** keeps pinned people first.
- **Select people** lets you **Confirm**, **Pin**, **Move to company**, **Add to** a project or series, or **Archive** several people at once.

## Companies

The Companies tab lists organizations. Each card shows the company's name, logo, relationship and how many contacts it has.

### Relationship types

Every company has one relationship to you:

**Client**, **Vendor**, **Partner**, **Prospect**, **Internal**, **Investor**, **Advisor** or **Other**.

The Companies tab shows **All** plus up to three relationship tabs. **Client**, **Vendor** and **Partner** are shown by default. Use **Filter types** to choose other ones, and **Set as default** to keep your choice.

### Add and edit a company

Click **Add company** and enter a **Name**, **Website**, **Relationship** and **Notes**.

Click a company to open its details:

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Details** | Website, relationship and notes. **Edit** changes them and lets you upload a logo. |
| **People** | Everyone linked to the company. **Add person** lets you pick someone you have (**Add an existing person…**) or **Name a new person**. |
| **Projects and series** | Projects and session series the company was added to. |
| **Related** | Linked knowledge items. |
| **Timeline** | Changes to this company over time. |

You can also link a project to a company in the project's settings. See [Projects](/knowledge/projects).

### Find and organize companies

- **Search companies…** finds companies by name.
- **Cards view** and **Sessions view** work like on the People tab.
- **Show archived** includes archived companies.
- **Select companies** lets you **Pin**, **Change relationship**, **Add to** a project or series, or **Archive** several at once.

## Link people and companies to your work

- **Sessions**: people are linked to a session through its speakers and participants. Rename participants on the session page.
- **Projects and series**: open a person's or company's menu and choose **Add to** → **Project** or **Session series**. A project's **People** and **Companies** tabs list them.
- **Memory cards**: a card's **About** section can link to people and companies. On the Memory cards tab, filter by **Person** or **Company** to see the cards about them.
- **Chats**: type **@** in a chat to mention a person or company, or attach them with **Add sources**.

## Archive

People and companies are archived, not deleted.

- Archiving a person (**Archive** in their menu) hides them from search and live context. Memory cards that mention them keep their links.
- Archiving a company hides it from search and live context. People keep their employer and history.

You can restore either one at any time. Turn on **Show archived**, then choose **Restore** from the card's menu.
