2. **Share Link Service:** Access to your plan's hosted sharing features on [talkbar.link](https://talkbar.link) (including unlimited active links, custom expiry, private links, email-gated sharing, and invitation emails, as applicable to your tier).

### 2.4 Upgrades & Renewals

- **Upgrades:** You may upgrade to a higher plan at any time by paying the applicable difference. Upgrading issues a new license key and starts a new 12-month period for updates and the share link service from the upgrade date.
- **Renewals:** Purchasing a renewal extends your Updates Period and Share Link Service by 12 months from the current end date.

Custom Branding (custom handle, business name, logo, colors, and footer links on shared pages and invitations) is included in the Business plan while its Share Link Service is active, and is available as an optional 12-month add-on for Personal, Plus, and Pro licenses. When Custom Branding expires without renewal, existing branded URLs continue to resolve using standard TalkBar branding.
