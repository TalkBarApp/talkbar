01Signature experience

The right context, exactly when it matters.

TalkBar listens for meaning, not noise. When prior memory, a document, a risk, or a better question can help, it surfaces a lightweight Live Context Card while the conversation is still happening.

Memory Card

Recall what was said last time, with source context and timestamp.

"Last call, Sarah said procurement approval was required before legal review."

Document Card

Surface the clause, memo, note, or PDF that matters right now.

"The signed agreement lists renewal notice at 60 days, not 30."

Suggested Question

Ask the better question before the moment passes.

"Ask whether budget approval has already been secured for Q3."

Fact Check Card

Flag a possible inconsistency between the conversation and prior records.

"This timeline differs from the March 12 discussion."

Blind Spot Card

Show what has not yet been clarified in the conversation.

"Timeline and decision owner have not been confirmed."

Action Card

Turn a live commitment into a next step you won't forget.

"Create task: send revised pricing model to Alex by Friday."

08FAQ

Common questions.

What exactly does TalkBar do?

TalkBar is a local-first, bot-free conversation intelligence app for Mac and Windows. It prepares you before important conversations, assists you with source-backed Live Context Cards while you talk, and turns conversations into searchable memory and clear follow-up.

How is it different from an AI note-taker?

Most AI note-takers summarize after the meeting. TalkBar helps before, during, and after — preparing private context, surfacing relevant memory live, and creating structured outputs afterward.

How does bot-free capture work?

TalkBar records from your desktop microphone and system audio instead of joining your meeting as a visible bot.

Can TalkBar run AI locally?

Yes. TalkBar supports local AI workflows through Ollama, allowing analysis to run on your own machine when Local Mode is enabled.

Can I choose what gets saved or deleted?

Yes. TalkBar gives you clear controls over what is recorded, retained, indexed, deleted, exported, or sent to any external provider.

What can I export?

Summaries, transcripts, action items, decisions, follow-up emails, and meeting briefs to formats such as Markdown, Word, PDF, and Obsidian-friendly folders.
